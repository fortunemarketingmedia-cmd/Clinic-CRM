# Dr. Revive CRM User Guide

## Welcome

This guide is for the **Dr. Revive** account. This account has full access to the Revive Clinic CRM, including patient records, clinic operations, reports, settings, forms, consent templates, lead scoring, and automations.

Use this guide as a practical walkthrough. You do not need to use every feature every day; start with the daily workflow, then use the relevant sections when needed.

> **Important:** Clinical notes, prescriptions, consents, photographs, and patient documents are sensitive medical records. Only record accurate information, check the selected patient and branch before saving, and never share your login.

---

## 1. Sign in and understand the screen

1. Open the CRM in your browser.
2. Sign in with the Dr. Revive account provided by your system administrator.
3. After signing in, the screen has three important areas:
   - **Left sidebar:** opens each CRM section.
   - **Top bar:** select the working clinic branch and sign out.
   - **Main area:** shows the page you selected.

### Select the correct branch first

Use the branch selector in the top navigation bar before working on leads, appointments, patients, or reports.

- Choose **All branches** when you want an overall Dr. Revive view.
- Choose a specific branch, such as **Nashik Road** or **Sharanpur Road**, when you are working only with that clinic.
- The selected branch affects the lists, counts, dashboard, and reports shown on screen.

Always check the branch before adding an appointment or updating a patient record.

---

## 2. Recommended daily workflow

Use this order at the beginning and end of each working day.

### Start of day

1. Open **Dashboard** to review today’s appointments, lead activity, and operational alerts.
2. Select the branch you are working in.
3. Open **Today’s Queue** to see who has arrived, is waiting, or needs the next action.
4. Open **Calendar** to review today’s schedule and resolve conflicts or missing assignments.
5. Open **Follow-ups** and **Tasks** to clear urgent work.
6. Review **Leads** for new enquiries, website leads, ad leads, and overdue contacts.

### During consultations

1. Open **Patients** and search for the patient.
2. Open the patient’s full record.
3. Review medical alerts, current medications, allergies, history, previous encounters, and treatment plan.
4. Record the consultation, treatment plan, procedure details, or prescription as required.
5. Sign and lock clinical documents only after checking them carefully.

### End of day

1. Review **Today’s Queue** for unfinished visits.
2. Complete or reschedule pending follow-ups and tasks.
3. Confirm important lead stages and next actions.
4. Review **Analytics** for appointments, lead conversion, and branch performance.
5. Check **Shared Inbox** for unanswered patient messages.

## 3. Dashboard

Open **Dashboard** from the sidebar for a quick clinic overview.

Use the dashboard to monitor:

- Lead volume and conversion.
- Appointments and appointment outcomes.
- Follow-ups that need attention.
- Patient and branch activity.
- High-level operational performance.

### Good practice

- Use a specific branch to manage one clinic’s day.
- Switch to **All branches** before reviewing total clinic performance.
- If a number looks unexpected, open the relevant page, such as Leads or Calendar, to review the individual records behind it.

---

## 4. Leads and enquiries

Open **Leads** to manage new enquiries from walk-ins, phone calls, WhatsApp, the website, Google Ads, and Meta Ads.

### Add a new lead

1. Click **Add lead**.
2. Select the correct branch.
3. Enter the name and mobile number. These are the most important fields.
4. Add email, address, treatment interest, source, priority, and follow-up information if available.
5. Save the lead.

The CRM automatically gives a new active lead an owner and an initial next action so it can be followed up.

### Find a lead

- Use the search field to search by name or mobile number.
- Use the status and source filters to narrow the list.
- Click a lead name or row to view the lead summary and timeline.

### Change a lead status

Use the status dropdown in the lead list. The CRM updates related pages automatically after a successful save.

Common stages:

| Stage | Use it when |
| --- | --- |
| New / Unassigned | The enquiry has arrived and is not yet handled. |
| Assigned | A team member owns the next action. |
| Attempting contact | Calls or messages have been started but no connection yet. |
| Connected | The person has responded or spoken with the clinic. |
| Qualified | The person is suitable and interested in proceeding. |
| Appointment proposed | A time is being discussed. |
| Appointment booked | A linked appointment has been created. |
| Nurturing | Follow-up will continue later. |
| Converted | The enquiry has become a patient. |
| Lost / Disqualified | The enquiry will not proceed. |

### Required information for some stages

The CRM protects data quality. Some stages require extra information:

- **Qualified:** enter qualification notes and a lead score.
- **Appointment booked:** create or link an appointment.
- **Lost:** record the reason.
- **Disqualified:** record the reason.

If the CRM displays a message under the status dropdown, complete the missing information in the lead record and save again.

### Book an appointment from a lead

1. In the lead row, click the calendar action.
2. Confirm the branch.
3. Choose date, time, appointment type, and resource or room if required.
4. Save the appointment.
5. Return to the lead and confirm the status is appropriate.

### Website leads

Website forms can create leads with source **Website**. Review these regularly, contact the person promptly, and update the stage after each meaningful interaction.

## 5. Follow-ups and tasks

### Follow-ups

Open **Follow-ups** to manage calls, messages, and contact commitments.

1. Select the correct branch.
2. Review overdue and due-today items first.
3. Open the related lead or patient when you need history.
4. Complete the follow-up only after recording the outcome where applicable.
5. Create the next action if more contact is needed.

### Tasks

Open **Tasks** for operational work assigned to the team.

- Use tasks for work that is not simply a patient contact, such as reviewing a case, confirming a supply decision, preparing a document, or checking a schedule.
- Keep the title clear and action-based.
- Use a realistic due time and priority.
- Mark tasks complete only when the work is done.

---

## 6. Appointments, queue, waitlist, and schedules

### Calendar

Open **Calendar** to create, review, reschedule, or update appointments.

1. Choose the branch and date.
2. Click the relevant action to add an appointment.
3. Choose the patient or lead, service, date/time, practitioner, and room/resource when needed.
4. Check for schedule or resource conflicts before saving.
5. Update the appointment status as the patient progresses through the visit.

### Today’s Queue

Open **Today’s Queue** for a live operational view of the selected branch.

Use it to move appointments through the correct operational stage, for example from scheduled to checked-in and then to the next appropriate state. Do not skip stages or mark a visit complete unless the required work has actually happened.

### Waitlist

Open **Waitlist** when a patient cannot get a suitable appointment time.

1. Add the person, service or request, preferred time range, and priority.
2. When a suitable slot becomes available, use the booking action from the waitlist.
3. Confirm the appointment details and update the waitlist status.

### Schedules & Rooms

Open **Schedules & Rooms** to review staff availability, working hours, treatment rooms, and equipment resources.

Use this before adding clinics, procedures, or resource-dependent appointments. Accurate schedules reduce double booking and room conflicts.

## 7. Patients and clinical record

Open **Patient Directory** to search, register, and manage patient records.

### Create a patient

1. Click **Create Patient**.
2. Select the correct branch.
3. Enter the patient’s identity and contact information carefully.
4. Add available medical and intake information.
5. Save the record.

Avoid duplicate records. Search by name and mobile number before creating a new patient.

### Open the patient’s full record

Click a patient to open their Patient 360 record. This is the main clinical workspace.

Review:

- Patient identity, branch, and primary concern.
- Medical history, medications, allergies, and clinical alerts.
- Appointments, encounters, treatment plans, procedures, prescriptions, files, forms, and consents.
- Previous notes and timeline entries.

### Update medical profile

1. Open the **Medical Profile** area.
2. Click **Update profile**.
3. Update history, medications, allergies, clinical alerts, and the reason for the change.
4. Mark a critical alert only when clinically appropriate.
5. Click **Save versioned update**.

The CRM preserves profile history, so document changes accurately.

### Record a consultation / encounter

1. In the patient’s clinical overview, choose **Start consultation**.
2. Record the complaint, examination, assessment, diagnosis, advice, treatment plan, and follow-up plan.
3. Save the encounter.
4. Review it carefully.
5. Use **Sign & lock** only when the entry is final.

After signing, use an addendum for a correction or later clarification. Do not alter the historical clinical record improperly.

### Create a treatment plan

1. Choose **Create treatment plan** from the patient record.
2. Document the concern, planned treatment, sessions, timing, and notes.
3. Save only after reviewing that it is clinically appropriate.
4. Use subsequent procedure sessions to record actual treatment delivery.

### Record a procedure

1. Choose **Record procedure**.
2. Enter the relevant procedure and clinical details.
3. Include device or treatment parameters where required.
4. Record any adverse event accurately.
5. Save the record.

### Prescriptions

1. Open the **Prescriptions** area in Patient 360.
2. Select **New prescription**.
3. Add medicines, strength, dosage, frequency, duration, and instructions.
4. Check allergies, contraindications, and the patient’s current medicines before signing.
5. Save and **Sign** the prescription when final.
6. Use **PDF** to provide or print the prescription.

## 8. Forms, consent, photographs, and documents

Open the relevant patient record and use the forms/files areas.

### Complete a patient form

1. Select a published form template.
2. Fill in the patient’s responses.
3. Review required fields.
4. Click **Submit versioned form**.

The submission history shows which version was completed and when.

### Capture consent

1. Select the correct published consent template.
2. Ensure the patient can read and understand the consent text.
3. Enter the signer name.
4. Capture the patient signature.
5. Add guardian and witness details when the template requires them.
6. Click **Sign consent**.

Use the PDF action to view the signed record. Withdraw a consent only with a documented reason and according to clinic policy.

### Clinical photographs

1. Use the clinical image upload area.
2. Choose the original image file.
3. Select Before, After, or Clinical Photograph.
4. Add angle, treatment area, and clinical annotation.
5. Mark marketing permission only if the patient has valid, signed marketing consent.
6. Upload the image.

Use before-and-after comparison only for properly identified images. Exported comparisons are watermarked.

### Secure documents

Use the document upload section for reports, prescriptions, consent documents, invoices, payment receipts, identity documents, and other supporting records.

Do not upload irrelevant documents or files belonging to another patient.

## 9. Communication centre and WhatsApp

Open **Shared Inbox** to work with official WhatsApp Cloud API conversations.

### Shared Inbox

1. Select the branch.
2. Use filters such as unread, unassigned, mine, or failed.
3. Select a conversation.
4. Review the linked patient or lead and message history.
5. Send a reply within the allowed service window, or add an internal note for staff.
6. Update the conversation status and priority when appropriate.

Internal notes are not sent to the patient.

### WhatsApp Templates

Use **WhatsApp Templates** to manage approved-message drafts and performance.

- Create clear, compliant templates using the required variable format.
- Sync provider status when a Meta integration is connected.
- Do not assume a draft is ready to send until it is approved by the provider.

### Broadcasts

Use **Broadcasts** for approved marketing communication only.

1. Choose an approved marketing template and sending number.
2. Build the audience.
3. Review the campaign details.
4. Request and complete the required approval steps.
5. Send only when appropriate.

The CRM applies consent filters, quiet-hour rules, and rate controls. Never attempt to bypass these safeguards.

## 10. Reports and analytics

Dr. Revive has access to **Analytics** under Reports.

1. Choose the branch or all branches.
2. Select the date range.
3. Review lead acquisition, lead funnel, appointment outcomes, patient activity, follow-up workload, collections, and branch performance.
4. Use the charts to identify issues, then open the operational page for the underlying records.

Useful questions to ask:

- Which lead source is producing the best enquiries?
- How quickly are new leads contacted?
- Which stages are causing conversion delays?
- Which branch has appointment or follow-up pressure?
- Are collections and billed revenue moving as expected?

Reports are decision-support tools. Verify unusual results against actual records before making a clinic decision.

## 11. Administration

Only Dr. Revive has the following administrative sections.

### Master Records

Open **Master Records** to review the clinic-wide patient directory and complete lead history. Use this for broad record review and quality checks.

### Lead Scoring

Open **Lead Scoring** to define or update lead score rules.

1. Create or edit rules only when the clinic has agreed on the scoring approach.
2. Enable a rule only after reviewing its conditions and points.
3. Recalculate scores if needed.
4. Review lead quality changes in the Leads page and Analytics.

### Automations

Open **Automations** to create clinic workflows.

1. Choose a clear trigger, such as a lead being created or an appointment being missed.
2. Define the workflow action and timing.
3. Keep new automations in test mode or inactive until reviewed.
4. Test with non-sensitive, controlled data.
5. Activate only when you understand the outcome and safeguards.

Avoid duplicate automations that create multiple tasks or messages for the same event.

### Forms & Consents

Use **Forms & Consents** to create and publish versioned templates.

For forms:

1. Create a stable template key and clear name.
2. Add fields, labels, options, required rules, and conditions.
3. Review the form carefully.
4. Publish only when ready.

For consents:

1. Use complete, approved legal and clinical language.
2. Set whether guardian or witness signatures are required.
3. Publish the template only after internal approval.

Published versions are historical records. Create a new version for changes instead of altering previous signed content.

### Settings

Open **Settings** for clinic-level configuration such as clinic identity, contact information, templates, and operational settings.

Make configuration changes carefully. They can affect documents, messages, and day-to-day workflows across all branches.

---

## 12. Access and security

Dr. Revive has full access. Receptionist users can use operational features but do not have access to Reports or Administration.

Security rules:

- Keep your password private.
- Sign out on shared computers.
- Do not use another person’s account.
- Verify branch and patient identity before saving.
- Do not export, download, or share patient records unless permitted by clinic policy.
- Use signed consent and appropriate permissions before marketing use of patient photographs.
- Report unexpected changes, access issues, or suspected account misuse immediately.

---

## 13. Troubleshooting

### I changed something but another page still looks old

The CRM refreshes connected pages after a successful save. If information still appears old:

1. Wait a few seconds.
2. Confirm the selected branch.
3. Refresh the browser page once.
4. Sign out and sign back in if the session is outdated.

### A status change does not save

Read the red message below the status control. Some lead stages require additional information, such as qualification notes, a score, a linked appointment, or a reason.

### I cannot find a patient or lead

1. Check the selected branch.
2. Search by mobile number as well as name.
3. Check whether the person is already a patient, lead, or both.

### I cannot access a feature

Confirm you are signed in as **Dr. Revive** and refresh the page. If access is still missing, contact the system administrator.
