# Receptionist CRM User Guide

## Welcome

This guide is for the **Receptionist** account in Revive Clinic CRM.

Your role is to keep the clinic’s day running smoothly: receive enquiries, manage leads and follow-ups, register patients, book appointments, update the queue, support communication, and keep records accurate.

You have access to daily CRM, front-desk, patient, and communication tools. You do **not** have access to the **Reports** or **Administration** sections. If you need changes to clinic settings, templates, scoring rules, forms, or automations, request them from Dr. Revive.

> **Important:** Patient information is confidential. Always confirm the patient, mobile number, and selected branch before creating or changing any record.

---

## 1. Sign in and choose your branch

1. Open the CRM in your browser.
2. Sign in with the Receptionist account provided by the clinic.
3. Use the branch dropdown in the upper navigation bar to select the branch you are currently working in.

You can choose between your assigned branches, for example **Nashik Road** and **Sharanpur Road**.

### Why the branch matters

The branch selection controls the leads, appointments, queue, patients, tasks, and WhatsApp conversations you see. Before adding an appointment or patient, always make sure the correct branch is selected.

---

## 2. Daily working routine

### At the start of the day

1. Select your branch.
2. Open **Dashboard** and check today’s activity.
3. Open **Today’s Queue** to see patients due today.
4. Open **Calendar** and review the day’s appointments.
5. Open **Follow-ups** and **Tasks** to identify urgent work.
6. Open **Leads** and contact new or overdue enquiries.
7. Check the **Shared Inbox** for unread WhatsApp conversations.

### During the day

- Register walk-in patients and new enquiries promptly.
- Update lead status after each meaningful contact.
- Keep appointment status and queue stage accurate.
- Record follow-up outcomes and create the next action.
- Escalate clinical questions, prescription requests, and sensitive cases to Dr. Revive.

### At the end of the day

1. Check that patient arrivals and appointment outcomes are updated.
2. Complete, reschedule, or reassign pending follow-ups.
3. Respond to or note any open patient messages.
4. Confirm tomorrow’s important appointments.
5. Inform Dr. Revive about urgent unresolved cases.

---

## 3. Dashboard

Open **Dashboard** for a quick view of the selected branch.

Use it to understand:

- New leads and follow-ups needing attention.
- Appointment activity.
- Patient and operational workload.
- Current clinic activity.

If a number looks unusual, open the related page—such as Leads, Calendar, or Today’s Queue—to see the individual records.

---

## 4. Leads and enquiries

Open **Leads** to manage people who contacted the clinic but may not yet be patients.

Lead sources can include walk-ins, phone calls, WhatsApp, website forms, Google Ads, and Meta Ads.

### Add a new lead

1. Click **Add lead**.
2. Confirm the branch.
3. Enter the person’s name and mobile number carefully.
4. Select the source, such as Walk In, Phone Call, WhatsApp, or Website.
5. Add treatment interest, follow-up details, priority, and notes if available.
6. Save the lead.

Before creating a lead, search by mobile number to avoid duplicates.

### Search and filter leads

- Search by name or mobile number.
- Filter by status or source.
- Use the selected branch to see the right branch’s enquiries.

### Update lead status

Update the status after every meaningful contact. This helps the clinic know exactly what must happen next.

| Status | When to use it |
| --- | --- |
| New / Unassigned | A new enquiry has arrived. |
| Assigned | The lead has an owner or next action. |
| Attempting contact | You have called or messaged but have not connected yet. |
| Connected | The person replied or spoke with the clinic. |
| Qualified | The person is suitable and interested in proceeding. |
| Appointment proposed | You are discussing an appointment time. |
| Appointment booked | A linked appointment has been created. |
| Nurturing | The person should be followed up later. |
| Converted | The lead became a patient. |
| Lost / Disqualified | The person will not proceed. |

### Important status rules

Some stages need extra information:

- **Qualified:** qualification notes and a score are required.
- **Appointment booked:** an appointment must be created or linked.
- **Lost:** a lost reason is required.
- **Disqualified:** a reason is required.

If a red message appears below the status selector, read it and enter the required information. Ask Dr. Revive when clinical judgement is required.

### Book an appointment from a lead

1. Find the lead.
2. Click the calendar action in the Actions column.
3. Confirm the branch and person details.
4. Choose date, time, appointment type, and room/resource if requested.
5. Save the appointment.
6. Confirm that the lead status is correct.

---

## 5. Follow-ups and tasks

### Follow-ups

Open **Follow-ups** to see calls, messages, and contact actions due for leads or patients.

1. Start with overdue items and items due today.
2. Open the related record if you need previous notes.
3. Contact the person through the approved channel.
4. Record the outcome accurately.
5. Complete the follow-up or create the next action.

Do not mark a follow-up complete if the contact was not made or the work is unfinished. Record the actual outcome instead.

### Tasks

Open **Tasks** for non-clinical operational work.

Examples:

- Confirming an appointment.
- Calling a patient about a reschedule.
- Preparing a form.
- Checking a room or schedule issue.

Keep task titles clear, use the correct priority, and mark them complete only when finished.

---

## 6. Calendar and appointment management

Open **Calendar** to book, review, reschedule, or update appointments.

### Create an appointment

1. Select the correct branch and date.
2. Click the appointment creation action.
3. Select the patient or lead.
4. Choose appointment date, time, type, service, practitioner, and resource/room as required.
5. Check the details with the patient.
6. Save.

### Reschedule or change an appointment

1. Open the appointment from the calendar.
2. Confirm that you opened the correct patient record.
3. Choose the updated date/time or status.
4. Save the changes.
5. Inform the patient through the approved communication channel when necessary.

### Avoid booking errors

- Check the selected branch.
- Confirm the date and time out loud with the patient.
- Check room and practitioner availability.
- Avoid double booking.
- Do not book a clinical procedure without the correct resource or required approval.

---

## 7. Today’s Queue

Open **Today’s Queue** to manage the live flow of patients at the selected branch.

Use it when patients arrive, wait, enter consultation, complete a visit, or require the next operational step.

### Queue best practice

1. Confirm the patient identity before checking them in.
2. Move the appointment through the correct stage only when it has actually happened.
3. If the patient does not arrive, follow the clinic’s missed-appointment process.
4. Escalate clinical or payment disputes to the appropriate person.

The queue helps Dr. Revive and the front desk see the real-time clinic workload. Keep it current.

---

## 8. Waitlist

Open **Waitlist** when a patient wants an appointment but no suitable slot is available.

### Add a patient to the waitlist

1. Select the person.
2. Enter the requested service or appointment need.
3. Add preferred date/time range and priority.
4. Save the waitlist entry.

### When a slot becomes available

1. Open the matching waitlist entry.
2. Contact the patient.
3. Use the booking action to create an appointment.
4. Confirm the appointment details.
5. Update the waitlist status.

---

## 9. Schedules and rooms

Open **Schedules & Rooms** to view staff availability, clinic hours, treatment rooms, and resources.

Use this page before booking appointments that require a practitioner, room, equipment, or special service.

If availability looks wrong, do not guess. Inform Dr. Revive so the schedule can be reviewed.

---

## 10. Patient Directory

Open **Patient Directory** to find, register, and review patients.

### Search before creating

Always search by:

- Mobile number.
- Patient name.

This reduces duplicate records and protects the quality of medical history.

### Register a new patient

1. Click **Create Patient**.
2. Select the correct branch.
3. Enter full name, mobile number, and contact information.
4. Add available registration and intake details.
5. Save.

Enter exactly what the patient provides. Do not create false clinical information or guess medical details.

### Update administrative details

You can update contact and registration details when the patient confirms them. For clinical history, medications, allergies, diagnosis, procedures, or prescriptions, follow clinic policy and ask Dr. Revive when necessary.

---

## 11. Patient record and Doctor Workspace

Open a patient record to view the Patient 360 information and related history.

You can use this view to:

- Confirm appointments and contact details.
- Review documented history and alerts before assisting the doctor.
- View forms, consent records, and uploaded documents as permitted.
- Support the doctor with operational preparation.

Open **Doctor Workspace** to see the current clinical queue and help direct patients to the correct next step.

### Clinical record safety

- Do not make up or alter clinical findings.
- Do not sign a consultation, procedure note, consent, or prescription for another person.
- Do not promise a diagnosis or treatment result to a patient.
- Escalate clinical questions to Dr. Revive.

---

## 12. Forms, consent, photographs, and documents

Within a patient record, you may need to help complete forms, collect consent, or upload documents.

### Patient forms

1. Select the correct published form.
2. Help the patient enter accurate answers.
3. Check required fields.
4. Submit the form.

### Consent

1. Select the correct consent template.
2. Ensure the patient has an opportunity to read and understand it.
3. Enter the patient’s name.
4. Capture the correct signature.
5. Add a guardian or staff witness only if required.
6. Sign and save the consent.

Never sign for a patient. Never use marketing permission unless valid consent is present.

### Documents and images

Upload only the correct patient’s document or image. Check the patient name before uploading.

For clinical photographs:

- Choose the correct type: Before, After, or Clinical Photograph.
- Record the treatment area and note when available.
- Marketing use requires proper signed permission.

---

## 13. Shared Inbox and WhatsApp

Open **Shared Inbox** to work with official WhatsApp conversations.

### Handle a conversation

1. Select the branch.
2. Use filters for unread, unassigned, mine, or failed conversations.
3. Open a conversation.
4. Confirm the linked patient or lead before replying.
5. Reply with accurate, approved information.
6. Update priority or status if needed.

### Internal notes

Use **Add note** for information that staff need to see but the patient must not receive.

Examples:

- “Patient requested a call after 6 PM.”
- “Waiting for doctor confirmation before replying.”
- “Patient sent an image; doctor review required.”

### WhatsApp Templates and Broadcasts

You can view and use the operational communication features available to your role. Do not create or send unapproved marketing content. Use approved templates and follow clinic policy for broadcasts.

For Meta connection issues, provider approval, template setup, or automation configuration, contact Dr. Revive.

---

## 14. Features not available to Receptionist

The following areas are intentionally restricted to Dr. Revive:

- **Reports / Analytics**
- **Master Records administration**
- **Lead Scoring rules**
- **Automations**
- **Forms & Consents template administration**
- **Clinic Settings**

If you need a report, template change, new automation, user change, or settings update, document the request and send it to Dr. Revive.

---

## 15. Security and privacy

Follow these rules every day:

- Keep your password private.
- Sign out when leaving a shared computer.
- Never access a patient record out of curiosity.
- Verify the patient and branch before saving changes.
- Do not share screenshots, documents, prescriptions, or photographs without approval.
- Do not use personal WhatsApp accounts for clinic communication.
- Report suspicious access, missing records, or unexpected changes immediately.

---

## 16. Troubleshooting

### I cannot see a lead, patient, or appointment

1. Check the branch selector in the top bar.
2. Search by mobile number as well as name.
3. Refresh the page once.
4. Ask Dr. Revive if the record may belong to another branch or needs review.

### A lead status does not change

Read the message below the status selector. Some stages require more information, such as qualification notes, an appointment, or a reason. Complete the required details or ask Dr. Revive.

### An appointment cannot be booked

Check that:

- The correct branch is selected.
- Date and time are valid.
- The practitioner and room/resource are available.
- The patient or lead is correct.

### I need access to a restricted feature

The restriction is by design. Contact Dr. Revive with your request rather than trying to work around it.
