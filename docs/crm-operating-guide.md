# Revive Clinic CRM operating guide

Updated: 23 July 2026

## Access model

The CRM has two effective access levels:

- **Admin** — manages clinic configuration and can view all branches or filter to one branch.
- **Receptionist** — performs daily clinic operations and sees only permitted branch data.

There is no patient login or patient portal. Clinic staff perform every action.

The Security, Audit Logs, and Ad Integrations management pages are intentionally unavailable to clinic users. Security controls and integration plumbing remain backend-only.

## Recommended daily workflow

1. Select the correct branch in the header. Admin may select **All branches**.
2. Open **Dashboard** to review today’s appointments, leads, follow-ups, and operational exceptions.
3. Open **Follow-ups** and complete overdue activities first.
4. Open **Today’s Queue** to manage expected, arrived, waiting, consultation, and treatment patients.
5. Use **Calendar** for new bookings, rescheduling, room allocation, and appointment status.
6. Use **Tasks** for internal work assigned to you.
7. Use **Shared Inbox** for WhatsApp conversations.
8. Admin reviews **Analytics** for performance and **Lead Scoring** for lead quality.

## Tasks versus follow-ups

### Tasks

A task is internal clinic work. Examples:

- verify a consent form;
- call an insurance provider;
- prepare a room;
- review a patient document;
- correct an incomplete record;
- perform work generated by an automation.

The Tasks page displays tasks assigned to the signed-in user. Click **Start** when work begins. Click **Complete**, enter completion notes, and save when finished. Completion notes create a useful handover for other staff.

Tasks appear after an admin, backend process, or automation creates and assigns them. A task will not appear for another receptionist unless it is assigned to that user.

### Follow-ups

A follow-up is a patient or lead communication activity. Examples:

- call a new enquiry;
- send an appointment reminder;
- ask whether the patient wants another consultation;
- follow up after a no-show;
- record a treatment-interest response.

Schedule a follow-up while creating or updating a lead. It then appears on the Follow-ups page for its owner and branch. Click **Record outcome**, enter what happened, and choose a resolution:

- **Closed** — no more action required.
- **Schedule next action** — enter the next action and date; the CRM creates a new open follow-up automatically.
- **Appointment booked** — the contact resulted in a booking.
- **Converted** — the lead became a patient.
- **Lost** or **Disqualified** — the enquiry will not continue.

Follow-up analytics become meaningful only after staff consistently record outcomes instead of leaving activities open.

## Page-by-page guide

### Login

Use the staff email and password. The secure refresh cookie keeps the session active. Admin and Receptionist permissions are applied automatically.

Data appears after the user is active and has branch access.

### Dashboard

The first operational summary after login.

It shows:

- patients and lead intake;
- today’s appointments;
- lead conversion;
- branch performance;
- appointment outcomes;
- today’s follow-ups and exceptions;
- upcoming appointment records.

Data appears immediately from live CRM records. Dashboard figures increase only when leads, patients, appointments, and follow-ups are actually recorded.

### Leads

The sales and enquiry pipeline.

Use it to:

- create enquiries;
- assign owners and branches;
- record source, priority, treatment interest, and contact details;
- schedule follow-ups;
- book appointments;
- move a lead through its lifecycle;
- detect duplicate mobile numbers or emails.

A lead appears after manual entry, QR registration, WhatsApp/backend capture, or another enabled backend source.

### Lead profile

Open a lead to see its full activity history, appointments, tasks, follow-ups, score, owner, and conversion status.

Use **Recalculate** when you want to refresh its score immediately. Normally, scoring already runs automatically after lead and appointment changes.

### Follow-ups

The communication work queue for leads and patients.

It displays open, overdue, and completed activity. Record every call/message outcome here. Selecting **Schedule next action** creates the next queue item and updates the lead’s next action.

Data appears after a lead follow-up is scheduled, an automation creates one, or a backend process creates one.

### Tasks

The signed-in user’s internal work queue.

Use **Start** when work begins and **Complete** with notes when finished. Use the lead link, when available, to open the related record.

Data appears only when tasks exist and are assigned to the current user. Automations can generate tasks.

### Calendar

The universal appointment calendar.

Use it to:

- create and edit appointments;
- select a branch or view all branches as Admin;
- filter by status, patient, or mobile;
- switch between calendar, day, room, and list views;
- allocate services, practitioners, rooms, and equipment;
- update appointment status.

The selected date has a strong primary-colour outline and filled date circle. The current date uses an amber treatment and **Today** badge, so today and the selected date remain distinct.

Appointments appear after they are created from the Calendar, lead workflow, waitlist, or backend.

### Today’s Queue

The front-desk live patient-flow board.

It tracks expected, arrived, waiting, consultation, treatment, and completed stages for the selected branch.

Data appears only for appointments dated today. Cancelled and no-show appointments are excluded. A branch must be selected because reception work is branch-specific.

### Waitlist

Use when a patient wants an appointment but the preferred slot, practitioner, or resource is unavailable.

Record the preferred date window, service, doctor, priority, and notes. When a slot becomes available, contact the person and convert the waitlist entry into a booking.

Data appears after staff add waitlist entries or another workflow creates them.

### Schedules & Rooms

The seven-day capacity view for practitioners, rooms, and equipment.

Admin **All branches** mode now shows:

- practitioners from every branch;
- each schedule with its branch name;
- all rooms and equipment with branch labels;
- combined appointments with a Branch column.

When one branch is selected, only that branch’s availability and bookings are shown.

Practitioner availability appears after staff schedules are configured. Rooms/equipment appear after clinic resources are configured. Booking counts appear after appointments use those resources.

### Patient Directory

The unique patient register.

Use it to search by name, mobile, or patient number and open the Patient 360 record.

A patient appears after a lead is converted/registered into a patient record. Repeated enquiries remain lead history rather than duplicate patients.

### Patient 360

The complete longitudinal patient record.

It contains demographics, medical profile, encounters, treatment plans, prescriptions, procedures, forms, consents, files, communications, tasks, and follow-ups.

Clinical information appears only after clinic staff record it. Empty sections mean that workflow has not yet been performed.

### Doctor Workspace

The clinical working area for consultations and treatment documentation.

Use it for encounters, diagnosis, treatment plans, procedures, prescriptions, clinical files, and signed/locked records.

Data appears after a patient and relevant appointment/encounter exist. Signed records become locked; later corrections use addendums.

### Shared Inbox

The clinic’s WhatsApp conversation workspace.

Use it to read conversations, reply, review message status, and work across branch-linked phone numbers.

Data appears after Meta/WhatsApp backend credentials, phone numbers, webhooks, and patient opt-in are configured and messages are received.

### WhatsApp Templates

Admin manages approved reusable message templates for confirmations, reminders, and follow-ups.

Templates appear after they are seeded or synchronized/configured through backend Meta setup. Provider approval may be required before sending.

### Broadcasts

Admin sends approved bulk WhatsApp communication.

Only opted-in recipients should be included. Large broadcasts may require approval. Delivery and failure status appears after worker processing and provider callbacks.

### Analytics

The clinic business-intelligence dashboard.

Tabs:

- **Executive** — lead intake, patients, appointments, collections, conversion, follow-ups, billed and outstanding amounts.
- **Leads** — trends, pipeline stages, sources, scoring quality, and priority.
- **Patients** — acquisition, active patients, status mix, and branch distribution.
- **Follow-ups** — workload trend, open/overdue/completed counts, channels, and activity types.
- **Financial** — invoices, billed, collected, outstanding, collection rate, invoice status, and payment-mode mix.
- **Branches** — leads, active leads, patients, appointments, invoices, and cross-branch comparisons.

Use date and branch filters. Admin can view all branches. Line, bar, and pie/donut charts use live CRM data.

Financial cards remain zero until your backend billing implementation writes invoice and payment records. The clinic-facing billing pages remain removed as requested.

### Master Records

Admin’s consolidated lead and patient database.

Use it for searching across branches and reviewing the unique patient master versus historical enquiries.

Data appears as soon as lead and patient records exist.

### Lead Scoring

Admin configures automatic scoring rules.

Available facts include source, priority, treatment interest, response/contact, appointment count, repeat enquiry count, and inactive days.

Score bands:

- Hot: 60 or more;
- Warm: 30–59;
- Cold: 0–29;
- Unqualified: below 0 or explicitly disqualified.

Creating, enabling, disabling, or changing a rule recalculates affected leads. **Recalculate all leads** performs a complete refresh.

### Automations

Admin configures backend operational rules such as creating tasks, scheduling messages, or triggering webhooks after CRM events.

Automation executions appear only after an enabled rule receives its configured trigger. External webhooks require a reachable endpoint and valid backend setup.

### Forms & Consents

Admin creates and publishes form templates, consent documents, and conditional fields.

Published forms can be completed from the patient workflow. Signed or submitted snapshots remain tied to the patient record.

Data appears after templates are published and a patient submission or consent is captured.

### Settings

Admin manages clinic profile, branch configuration, operational preferences, services, resources, and other master settings.

Configuration changes determine what becomes available in appointment, schedule, room, patient, and communication workflows.

### Users / Staff access

Admin creates and manages clinic users and branch access.

Choose only one effective access level:

- Admin for configuration and all-branch management.
- Receptionist for daily operations and permitted branches.

Inactive users cannot log in. Staff only see branch data after branch access is assigned.

### Public QR registration

The QR link is a public clinic intake form, not a patient portal.

The patient or receptionist can enter enquiry details. Submission creates/updates the clinic-side lead workflow. Duplicate checks prevent unnecessary repeated identities.

## When dashboards look empty

An empty card or zero chart is normally a data-state signal:

- no leads — no enquiries entered or captured in the selected period;
- no patients — no leads converted/registered;
- no appointments — no bookings in the selected date range;
- no follow-ups — staff have not scheduled durable lead follow-ups;
- no tasks — nothing assigned to the current user;
- no staff schedules — practitioner availability not configured;
- no room bookings — appointments have not been assigned to resources;
- no WhatsApp data — provider credentials/webhooks/messages not configured;
- zero financial values — backend billing has not written invoices/payments;
- no chart data — branch/date filters exclude existing records.

Before treating zero as an error, reset the date range, select **All branches** as Admin, and confirm the underlying records exist on their operational page.
